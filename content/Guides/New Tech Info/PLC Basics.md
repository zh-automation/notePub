# Why is it a PLC?
Why isn't it a PC? It is just a computer. It has all the same basic components: CPU, RAM, storage, etc. So why can't a PC be a PLC? Well, it can and it often is, but even the PCs in industrial environments are even considered separate from their peers. They're usually labeled IPCs for industrial PC
