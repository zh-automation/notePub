## Word
A logical grouping of data one [[Register|register]] long, often used as a [[Datatype|datatype]] in PLC languages. The size is dependent on the CPU. However, in industrial automation terms, a word is often 16 [[Bit|bits]]. Most industrial uses have a word represented in binary rather than in decimal/integer form.
