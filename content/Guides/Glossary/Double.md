## Double
A double word is a [[Datatype|datatype]] usually holding values 2 [[Word|words]] long. Often, in industrial automation, this is represented by 32 [[Bit|bits]] of data. Usually abbreviated as Double or DWORD. 
A double can sometimes also be a double precision float, that is, a float with ~15 decimal digits of precision.
The context of use is important.
